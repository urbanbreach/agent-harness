Request a fresh context window. Once the current tool calls finish, the harness rolls the conversation over: older messages leave the live window, and your persistent notes, the latest request and the most recent messages are carried forward. The full history stays readable at `history://current/full`.

This tool takes no arguments, and it does not compact the conversation or change the transcript itself. Bring your notes up to date with `context_notes` before you call it, because the new window starts from the notebook rather than from remembered detail.
