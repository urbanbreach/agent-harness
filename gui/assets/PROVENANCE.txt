Bundled GUI assets

Fonts
JetBrainsMono-Regular.ttf, JetBrainsMono-Bold.ttf, JetBrainsMono-Italic.ttf,
JetBrainsMono-BoldItalic.ttf: official JetBrains Mono upstream TTF files.
Source: https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono/tree/master/fonts/ttf
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1, retained in fonts/OFL.txt.

Outline icons
Stock SVG files are vendored from lucide-static 0.468.0:
https://registry.npmjs.org/lucide-static/-/lucide-static-0.468.0.tgz
License and upstream notices are retained in icons/LICENSE.
Stock aliases: alert = triangle-alert, fork = split, rewind = history,
sparkle = asterisk, compose = square-pen.
Stock outline strokes use 2 viewBox units, except separately calibrated
file-diff (2.2) and cloud-upload (1.8) masks. At 14 px and 2x scale, the
standard stroke is 2.33 physical pixels. The panel-toggle rectangles use
corner radius 3. The sparkle alias enlarges the stock asterisk paths by 1.5
around the viewBox center with inverse stroke scaling, retaining the
rendered 2-unit stroke.
These names intentionally select the documented shapes rather than the
similarly named but differently shaped Lucide glyphs.

Custom artwork
The appearance, changes, queue, block, stop, stop-filled, terminal-square,
cursor-spark, file-bottom-left-arrow, arrow-left, arrow-right,
plus, search, folder, folder-open, window-maximize,
window-minimize, window-restore, and the provider-claude,
provider-deepseek, provider-grok SVG files are original vector geometry authored
for Harness from the clean-room shape descriptions.
No upstream product SVG source was used for these drawings.
The custom provider silhouettes above are original interpretations, not
official brand assets.

star.svg and star-filled.svg are original mathematical five-point stars.
Their ten vertices alternate outer radius 10 and inner radius 5.5 around
center (12,12), at angles -90 + 36*i degrees for i=0..9, rounded to three
decimal places. Both use stroke-width 2; the filled variant fills currentColor.
No screenshot contours or pixel-traced geometry are used.
queue.svg is an original top bar, quarter-circle turn, and arrow drawing.

command.svg is the unmodified Framework7 Icons command silhouette:
https://github.com/framework7io/framework7-icons/blob/master/package/svg/command.svg
License: MIT, copyright Vladimir Kharlampidi 2016; notice retained in the SVG.

folder-new.svg uses the Framework7 Icons folder_badge_plus silhouette:
https://github.com/framework7io/framework7-icons/blob/master/package/svg/folder_badge_plus.svg
License: MIT, copyright Vladimir Kharlampidi 2016; notice retained in the SVG.

provider-openai.svg uses the canonical 256x260 provider-identification mark
published in SVGCode's brand-logos catalog:
https://svgcode.com/icons/brand-logos/openai
Published icon license: MIT; publisher attribution and full notice retained
in the SVG. The mask uses currentColor; OpenAI retains its trademarks.

File icons
The full-color SVGs are unmodified stock Material Icon Theme artwork:
https://github.com/material-extensions/vscode-material-icon-theme/tree/main/icons
License: MIT, retained in file-icons/LICENSE.
Each file uses the matching upstream filename except file.svg, which is
upstream document.svg. Two exceptions are original Harness drawings:
rust.svg (a gear with computed teeth around an R) and text.svg (a page
outline with a folded corner). Render file icons as full-color images, not
masks.
All shipping assets are local; displaying them needs no network access.
